About Us

Established in 2012 NATURAL STONE TRADERS INC. Is an emerging leader in the supply of ethically sourced natural stone landscape products in different parts of the world.

NATURAL STONE TRADERS INC. Is based in Toronto, Canada & has its operations spread out to most parts of Canada & North America.

We at NATURAL STONE TRADERS INC. Offer an unbeatable selection of product range, sizes, colors, textures and much more for a wide variety of applications - ALL SOURCE RESPONSIBLY & ETHICALLY.
